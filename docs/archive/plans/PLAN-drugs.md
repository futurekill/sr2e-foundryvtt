# Plan: Drugs and toxins (Shadowtech p.85–100)
_Round 2 — LEAN CORE + Codex R2 amendments at the end. Round 1 was: LEAN CORE (the user's choice after Codex R1): automation limited to
doses, attribute effects, toxin damage and the addiction tests; the rest are card notes_

## Rules (read from the rendered pages)
- **Conversion**: core p.277 says SR II "does not change any information given
  in Shadowtech". Every code here has first-edition Staging 2, which is SR II's
  fixed staging; the critter conversion rule (p.278) adds Power only for
  Staging 3 or 4. So the codes carry over as printed: 5D2 → 5D, 4S2 Stun →
  4S Stun, 10L2 Stun → 10L Stun, 4D2 → 4D.
- **Substance use (p.87)**:
  - Every drug has an **Addiction** rating (P physical: Body test; M mental:
    Willpower test), a **Tolerance** rating and a **Strength** rating.
  - Addiction and tolerance tests are made **after the drug wears off**. For
    addiction: a Resistance Test, the attribute vs the Addiction Rating, and
    one success means not addicted (a separate test for each of P and M).
  - For tolerance: a Body Resistance Test vs the Tolerance Rating; 0 successes
    means permanently immune to the drug.
  - Every Strength-th dose adds +1 to both ratings. Withdrawal, the weekly
    Essence loss and "kicking the habit" are long-term GM bookkeeping.
- **Stimulant overuse (p.85)**: a second dose before the first wears off gives
  a Light **Mental** (Stun) wound, and the new dose works at half: bonuses
  halved (round down), penalties unchanged.
- **The compounds** (p.95–100):

  | Compound | Effect | Duration | Data |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | ACTH (p.95) | triggers the adrenal pump (bioware) | — | Add 0, Tol 2, Str 25; 100¥ / 6-dose inhaler; Legal, 5/12 h, SI 1 |
  | Atropine (p.96) | 5D (Physical) damage at once and again every 15 min until neutralised or filtered; +1 TN to all Active Skills (+1 more for melee and close-range firearms), +2 to Knowledge, Technical, B/R, Language and Magic skills | until treated | Injection; 600¥/dose; Legal, 5/12 h, SI 1 |
  | Cyanide (p.97) | 4D (Physical) | immediate if inhaled, 1 minute otherwise | Air, ingestion, injection; 360¥/dose; Legal, 3/48 h, SI .5 |
  | Hyper (p.98) | 4S Stun + vertigo (+1 all TNs); +4 to concentration tasks (spellcasting included); any damage taken adds half its boxes (round up) as extra Stun | 60 min − 5 min per Body success | Air, injection; 180¥; 4-M1, 4/24 h, SI .9 |
  | Kamikaze (p.99) | +1 Body, +1 Quickness, +2 Strength, +1 Willpower, +1D6 Initiative; negates the first 4 boxes of damage taken (any mix of Physical and Mental) | 10 × 1D6 minutes | Add 4P, Tol 2, Str 4; 50¥; 3-M1, 5/4 days, SI 5 |
  | MAO (p.100) | 10L Stun; −1 Reaction, −1D6 Initiative (no defence); weakens an active adrenal pump | 10 turns − Body successes | Injection; 280¥/dose, injector 320¥ for 6 (no Stun); 4-M1, 5/36 h, SI 2 |

  Kamikaze's long-term effects are also printed: every 4 applications cost a
  box off both monitors' maximum, and after (Body ÷ 2) uses, cyberware and
  bioware stop working.
- **Ares Squirt** (p.92): the delivery weapon. Light, Conceal 7, Ammo
  10(cartridge)/20(gel), Damage Special, 1.75 kg, 750¥; refill 250¥.

## Design (lean core — chosen by the user)
Automated:
- spending a dose;
- the attribute effects (Kamikaze, MAO) for a rolled duration;
- toxin damage;
- the addiction and tolerance tests.

Everything else is a **card note** for the GM, and stated as such:
- Kamikaze's 4-box absorption and long-term wasting;
- Hyper's overload damage, its +1 all TNs and +4 on concentration;
- Atropine's skill-group TNs;
- stimulant overuse;
- MAO and ACTH with the adrenal pump;
- Cyanide's onset delay.

### Content (sr2e-shadowtech, generated by a tools/gen script)
- Six drug gear items (category `drug`), plus **MAO Injector (6 doses)** as
  its own item (320¥, no Stun attack), and the **Ares Squirt** weapon.
  - `quantity` counts **doses**: ACTH is an inhaler of 6 (100¥ for the six).
  - The generator keeps stable ids and the page citations.
- `flags.sr2e.drug` holds:
  - `key` (canonical compound id);
  - `damage` `{power, level, type}` or none;
  - `repeatMinutes` (Atropine 15);
  - `duration`, one of `{minutes: "10*1d6"}`, `{minutes: 60,
    bodyReducesBy: 5}`, `{turns: 10, bodyReducesBy: 1}` or none;
  - `addiction` `{rating, P: bool, M: bool}` or none;
  - `tolerance`, `strength`;
  - `notes` (the card notes above).
- **Attribute effects** are Active Effects on the item (transfer false), with
  numeric **ADD** changes only:
  - Kamikaze: body/quickness/strength/willpower `.mod`, and `initiative.mod
    +1` (extra dice);
  - MAO: `reaction.mod −1`, `initiative.mod −1`.

### System (sr2e)
1. **NPC Initiative dice**: `NPCData.prepareDerivedData` adds
   `initiative.mod` to the dice, as characters do (the field was unused on
   NPCs; spirits are untouched), before the projecting override.
2. **Use a dose**: a button on drug rows in the character gear tab and the NPC
   gear list (owner only; characters and NPCs only).
   - In a per-actor local queue (`enqueueAttack("dose:" + actorUuid)`): re-read
     the item, refuse at quantity 0.
   - Roll the duration **first**: `10*1d6`, or a **Body Success Test at TN 4**
     (the book gives no TN; 4 is SR II's default, flagged as a reading), where
     each success cuts `bodyReducesBy`, floored at 0. A zero duration means
     only the damage applies.
   - Then decrement the quantity and create one actor Active Effect copying
     the item's effects. It gets `origin` = the item uuid, `name` = the
     compound, and `flags.sr2e.drugEffect = {key, exposureId}`. Duration:
     minutes → `duration.seconds` + `startTime` (world time); **turns →
     `duration.rounds`** plus the started combat's id, round and turn (one
     SR II Combat Turn = one Foundry round, as in SR2ECombat). Turns with no
     combat fall back to `seconds = turns × 3`. It is made only if the item has
     effects.
   - Post the card:
     - the rolled duration;
     - the damage button;
     - the notes;
     - the addiction/tolerance button (only when the drug has those ratings);
     - a warning when the same `key` is already active: "a second dose before
       the first wears off — Light Stun and half effect (p.85), GM".
3. **Toxin damage**: a small dedicated resist, not the combat dialog.
   - `resistToxin(actor, card)` rolls **Body** (no Combat Pool, no armour, no
     misc dice) vs TN = Power. Every 2 successes stage down one level
     (p.112 resistance staging). The result is applied with
     `damageUpdate(type, boxes)` in ONE update.
   - The card is flag-backed: `flags.sr2e.toxin = {actorUuid, exposureId, seq,
     power, level, type}`, resolved once through the `resolves` marker, with
     `beforeRoll` re-checks and the owner/GM check. Unlinked tokens use the
     token actor uuid snapshotted at dose time.
   - Atropine: the card has "15 minutes pass: next damage" (GM), which posts a
     new toxin card with seq + 1.
4. **Addiction and tolerance** (after it wears off, p.87): a button rolls a
   Body (P) and/or Willpower (M) Resistance Test vs the Addiction Rating (0
   successes = addicted), and a Body test vs the Tolerance Rating (0 successes
   = immune). A small dialog first lets the GM raise the ratings for the
   Strength-based growth they track. The results are reported, not stored.
5. **Active drugs list** on the character and NPC sheets: each active drug
   effect with its remaining time (from `duration.remaining`; "expired" when
   ≤ 0 and finite; "until ended" when there is no duration). An **End**
   button deletes the effect (owner). Nothing auto-deletes or auto-suppresses.
   Open actor sheets re-render on `updateWorldTime` and `updateCombat` (read
   only) so the remaining time stays current.

## Tests
- Unit:
  - duration: `10*1d6` bounds; 60 − 5 × successes floored at 0; 10 − successes;
  - toxin staging: 5D with 2 or 4 successes → S or M; 6 → L; 8 → none.
- Quench:
  - Kamikaze on a character: quantity −1; Body +1, Quickness +1, Strength
    +2, Willpower +1, and +1 Initiative die; End removes it and the values
    return.
  - The same on an NPC, and on an unlinked token actor (its own effect).
  - A second dose while active shows the warning.
  - MAO: Reaction −1 and Initiative dice −1; in a started combat the effect
    has `duration.rounds` = 10 − successes.
  - Atropine: the toxin card resolves once (a second click is refused); Body
    only (no Combat Pool offered); "next damage" makes a new card.
  - Use at quantity 0 is refused; a cancelled step spends nothing.
  - The addiction/tolerance button for Kamikaze reports addicted and immune.
- Content: `npm run validate` in sr2e-shadowtech passes; the generator is
  re-runnable (stable ids).

## Round 2 amendments (Codex R2, all accepted; #2 as a stated limit)
- **Wording**: the attribute effects have a *displayed* duration and are
  **ended manually**. Expired effects stay visible (as "expired") until
  removed.
- **Toxin resolves once**:
  - `resistToxin` runs in a per-actor local queue. It checks the actor's
    `flags.sr2e.toxinDone["<exposureId>_<seq>"]` inside the queue.
  - It commits the damage (`damageUpdate`) **and** that completion flag in ONE
    actor update; a fully resisted result writes the flag alone.
  - The chat `resolves` marker is informational; the actor flag is the
    authority.
- **Cross-client (#2) — a stated limit**, as for spirit services: one person
  drives an actor. The owner and the GM acting on the same actor at the same
  instant is Foundry last-write-wins. The per-actor local queue covers this
  client, including double clicks.
- **The dose flow is ordered so a failure never spends a dose without
  delivering it**:
  1. assign the `exposureId`;
  2. create the actor effect, tagged with it;
  3. decrement the quantity;
  4. post the card.

  If step 3 fails, the dose wasn't spent (favourable, reported). If step 4
  fails, the active-drugs row offers **Re-post card** for that exposure
  (idempotent: it finds or recreates the card by `exposureId`). Drugs with no
  effect still get a lightweight tracking entry (see the list), so a failed
  card is recoverable.
- **Atropine repeat is idempotent**: "next damage" makes the `seq + 1` card
  only if no card with `(actorUuid, exposureId, seq + 1)` exists. It then
  marks its own card's repeat as used (a flag, re-rendered with the button
  gone).
- **Roll options**: toxin, addiction and tolerance rolls use
  `rollSuccessTest` with `isResistance: true`. Every Body roll (toxin,
  tolerance, physical addiction, and the duration tests) also passes
  `this._bodyTestOpts()` (biosystem overstress). The duration test is a
  Body *Success* Test, not a resistance test, so wound penalties apply.
- **Sell-back by doses**: drug items record `acquiredQuantity` and
  `acquiredListValue` at purchase, as ammo does. `onSellItem` prorates drug
  items with `proportionalRefund` (empty = 0¥).
- Tests added:
  - the toxin completion flag stops a second resolution (sequential double
    click);
  - fully resisted writes the flag;
  - a second "next damage" click makes no duplicate;
  - a simulated decrement failure leaves the effect and the dose (reported);
  - Re-post card;
  - selling a half-used ACTH inhaler refunds half; an empty one refunds 0.
- (R3) **Repeat creation is queued too**: the successor lookup, card creation
  and parent marking run in the same per-actor local queue as the toxin
  resist and dose. The test fires two repeat requests with `Promise.all`, and
  exactly one successor exists.

## Implementation notes (built)
- A zero duration (fully shrugged off) creates the tracking effect with no
  changes, listed as expired.
- MAO's rounds come from the started combat that contains THIS actor;
  otherwise turns × 3 seconds.
- A card's identity is `(actorUuid, exposureId, seq)`. Re-post binds to the
  current actor (a token copied from a dosed actor gets its own card).
- `chargenItemCost` prices drug packs per package, as it does ammo.
- The implementation was reviewed by Codex over 2 rounds (approved).
