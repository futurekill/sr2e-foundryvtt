**SR2E for Foundry — what's new** 2/3

**Chrome & bodies**
• Second-hand cyberware: half price, same Essence. Once per run, when it really matters, the GM can call its Resistance Test. A part that fails stops working. 😬
• Shapeshifters are playable (Bear, Fox, Leopard, Seal, Tiger, Wolf), with forms, regeneration and silver.
• Some Edges and Flaws now apply their effects on your sheet by themselves.
• Raising an attribute above racial max with Karma (double cost, GM's OK) works properly.

**Guns & gear**
• Tracer rounds (Fields of Fire p.51) for full-auto weapons.
• Smoke grenades make a visible 10 m cloud.
• Skill cleanup ⚠️: **Launch Weapons, Pilot and Subsonic ammo are gone** (no book prints them). Grenade launchers use Firearms; rockets, missiles and mortars use Gunnery. If your sheet has "Pilot", swap it for the real vehicle skill: Car, Bike, Winged Plane, Rotor Craft, Hovercraft, Motorboat, Sailboat or Vectored Thrust.
• Items now record their book and page.

**Vehicles**
• The core vehicles have their real core-book stats again (drag them in fresh to update).
• Ramming is an opposed card, and Crash Tests get asked for when the rules need them. Occupants resist crashes from the card.
• Damage to vehicles counts their armor mods, and called shots with Light weapons can hurt them.
• New Vehicle Workshop: design a vehicle or customize one, and mods change its stats.
