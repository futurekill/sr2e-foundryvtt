**SR2E for Foundry — what's new since v0.117.1** (now v0.160.0) 🎲 1/3

**Magic**
• Combat spells now work like gunfights: each target has its own TN, cover, visibility and line of sight. A tie does the spell's base damage, and you can split Force across several targets (p.129).
• A 0-success cast still posts a card, so Karma can save it. An all-ones cast is a misfire (+2 Drain TN), with an "Avoid an Oops" offer first.
• Area spells: you see the area before you click. Esc no longer opens the main menu.
• Any magician can add unused Magic Pool when resisting a spell.
• Rat shamans get their +2 dice for Spirits of Man, and the spirit sheet shows the attack the spirit really makes.

**Healing & wounds**
• Healing was checked line by line against p.111–115 and fixed.
• With Calendaria on, healing takes game time: Heal, First Aid and Recover Stun finish on their own when the time passes. Past the Physical column you bleed a box every 10 minutes until someone stabilizes you. Don't bleed out. 🩸
• A Deadly wound asks for the permanent-damage roll (p.113): a lost organ point or a mangled limb if it goes badly.
