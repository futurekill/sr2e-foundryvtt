# Contributing to Shadowrun 2nd Edition

Thanks for helping. This project takes **issues only** for now: we don't accept pull requests. Every change is made,
tested and released by the maintainer, and an issue is the best way to get one made.

Everyone who takes part follows the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/futurekill/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Report a problem or an idea
Open an issue with one of the forms: [new issue](https://github.com/futurekill/sr2e-foundryvtt/issues/new/choose).

- **Bug report** — something that doesn't work. Give your Foundry version, the SR2E system version, and the other modules you have turned on; the steps that cause it; what you expected and what
  happened. Errors from the browser console (F12) help a lot.
- **Wrong or missing book content** — a number, rule or entry that doesn't match the book. Give the **book**, the
  **printing** if you know it, the **page**, and what the page prints. For the core book, the reference is the
  corrected 11th printing.
- **Feature request** — something new you would like.

Problems with dice, sheets, combat, magic, the Matrix or vehicles belong here. A problem that only happens with a content module turned on belongs in that module's tracker. Before you report a bug, the [QA test plan](docs/QA-PLAN.md) may already describe the expected behaviour. The [wiki](https://github.com/futurekill/sr2e-foundryvtt/wiki) has the how-to guides.

**Security problems:** never in a public issue. See the
[Security policy](https://github.com/futurekill/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Rules and books
- **Rules as written.** The project follows the books as printed. Where two books (or two pages) disagree, or a book
  leaves a gap, the decision is written on the wiki's [Rulings](https://github.com/futurekill/sr2e-foundryvtt/wiki/Rulings) page (one section per book). A report that a ruling is wrong is welcome:
  say which ruling, and cite the page that supports you.
- **Don't paste book text.** The Shadowrun books are copyrighted works. The project records game data (names,
  numbers, costs) with page citations, not the books' prose. In an issue, a page number and the value printed there
  are enough; quote at most a short phrase.
- **No scans or book art.** Don't attach pages, scans or art from the books. The art in these repositories is
  original.

## What happens to an issue
The maintainer checks issues regularly. A fix is released, and the issue gets a reply naming the release. The issue
stays open until you confirm the fix works for you, so please come back and say so.
